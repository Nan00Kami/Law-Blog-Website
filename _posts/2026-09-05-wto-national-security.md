The invocation of Article XXI of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT 1994) has transformed from a rare diplomatic defense into a routine instrument of economic statecraft.

## The Standard of Review

Historically, states treated Article XXI(b) as entirely self-judging. However, the Panel ruling in *Russia – Measures Concerning Traffic in Transit* established that while the defending Member defines its essential security interests, the determination of whether an "emergency in international relations" exists remains subject to objective review.

> "An emergency in international relations refers generally to a situation of armed conflict, or of latent armed conflict, or of heightened tension or crisis..."

## Implications for Multilateralism

As states increasingly conflate economic resilience with national security, the line between valid trade restrictions and unilateral protectionism continues to blur.
